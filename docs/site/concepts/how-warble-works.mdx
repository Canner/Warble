## What this page will cover

- The three parts — front-end compiler (`core/`, sans-IO Rust), per-target back-ends/dispatchers, and the future UI — and the seams between them (IR JSON, capability manifest).
- Why the IR is "the seam": every back-end consumes the same `ir.json`, which keeps back-ends thin and swappable.
- The zero-wren boundary: `core/` and components stay transitively wren-free; only the MDL adapter depends on `wren-core-base`.
