## What this page will cover

- The four capability resolution modes and the criticality rule: safety-critical capabilities never silently degrade (e.g. `human_approval` loud-fails on a headless target).
- `locked: true` guardrails (e.g. `read_only_execution`) as a compile-time loud-fail if a profile tries to weaken them.
- The capability manifest — the runtime-agnostic advertisement projected from the IR that a meta-harness can use to call a profile without absorbing its execution.
