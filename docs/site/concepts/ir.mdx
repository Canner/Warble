## What this page will cover

- What the IR carries: resolved prompts, per-step tier + I/O contract, guardrails, render contract, required capabilities.
- Why the IR is runtime-agnostic — no mechanism names (cron, subagent, Slack, …) leak in; those resolve later via `realize-via`.
- The three orthogonal enums back-ends dispatch on: `realization_kind`, `outcome.kind`, `trigger.kind`.
